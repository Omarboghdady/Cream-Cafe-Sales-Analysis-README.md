Cream Café | Sales Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview

An interactive sales analysis project developed using Power BI and SQL/MySQL to explore café sales performance, identify revenue trends, evaluate product category performance, and understand customer purchasing patterns throughout the day.

The project uses a publicly available dataset and focuses on turning sales data into meaningful business insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

Tools & Technologies

- Power BI: Interactive dashboards, KPI cards, and data visualization.
- SQL / MySQL: Limited SQL querying and data exploration.
- Dataset: Publicly available café sales dataset.

Key Insights

- Total Revenue: Approximately 698.8K across the displayed reporting period.
- Monthly Growth: Revenue increased from 76K in February to 166K in June, making June the strongest month shown.
- Leading Category: Coffee generated approximately 270K in revenue, followed by Tea at approximately 196K.
- Peak Sales Hour: 10:00 AM recorded the highest hourly sales value in the displayed hourly analysis.
- Top Product: Sustainably Grown Organic Lg ranked first among the displayed products, generating approximately 21.2K in revenue.
- Store Performance: Revenue was distributed relatively evenly across the three displayed store locations, with each contributing approximately one-third of total sales.

Business Recommendations

- Optimize Inventory: Prioritize the availability of high-revenue categories while monitoring demand for lower-performing categories.
- Improve Peak-Hour Operations: Review staffing and preparation capacity around the busiest morning hours.
- Investigate Monthly Growth: Examine the factors behind the increase in revenue from February through June to identify repeatable growth opportunities.
- Evaluate Product Performance: Review pricing, promotions, and product placement to support sales across different product categories.
- Compare Store Performance: Investigate differences in store-level sales and identify opportunities to improve performance at each location.

Project Objective

The objective of this project is to demonstrate practical sales analysis, interactive dashboard development, and the use of business insights to support better operational and commercial decisions.
